## The premise, challenged

Stated problem: "the legal pages point at an unowned domain, fix it."

Actual problem: Gituas has three identities and uses the wrong one everywhere
that matters.

| Where | Domain |
|---|---|
| Privacy / Terms / Data Deletion pages | `gituas.app` — **nobody owns it** |
| Meta app: App domain, Site URL, privacy, terms, data-deletion | `gituas.vercel.app` |
| TikTok app: website, privacy, terms + today's audit submission | `gituas.vercel.app` |
| What we actually own and have wired | `gituas.com` |

`gituas.com` is already live on Vercel (200 on `/` and `/privacy`) with Cloudflare
Email Routing MX records in place. The domain was bought, pointed, and then never
adopted.

This is the same rule TikTok rejected us on the first time:
> "the email domain should match the company website domain in profile"

## What would happen if we did nothing

A Meta reviewer opens the Data Deletion page — a required callback — and finds an
address that bounces. A customer exercising a GDPR right writes to a void. Neither
we nor they ever find out. That is a silent failure on the one page whose entire
purpose is to be actionable.

## Approaches considered

APPROACH A: hello@gituas.com only
  Effort: XS · Risk: Low · Completeness: 5/10
  Swap all three addresses to the mailbox we know is configured.
  Pro: cannot fail — that route exists. Con: no role separation.

APPROACH B: role addresses (privacy@ / support@)
  Effort: S · Risk: Low-Med · Completeness: 7/10
  Better long-term shape. Depends on Cloudflare routes we cannot verify from here.
  An unrouted `privacy@` recreates the same silent bounce with a tidier name.

APPROACH C: full consolidation onto gituas.com now
  Effort: M · Risk: HIGH today · Completeness: 10/10
  Right destination, wrong moment. Today's TikTok submission ships a demo video of
  `gituas.vercel.app`, and TikTok requires the video's domain to match the profile
  website. Changing it now contradicts our own evidence. Meta's behaviour when app
  settings change mid-review is undocumented — treat as a one-way door.

DECISION: **A now, C after both verdicts.** B is a one-line follow-up once the
Cloudflare routes are confirmed.
